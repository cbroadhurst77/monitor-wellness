What it does

Day/Night schedule

Color temperature and brightness follow sunrise and sunset at your actual location, smoothly interpolated — not a jarring clock-time cutover.

Migraine Mode

One click, hotkey, or tray-icon press applies an instant, muted green tint based on published migraine-photophobia research — then always fades back off slowly, never abruptly.

Per-monitor control

Exclude a reference display, nudge one panel's color independently, or dim monitors differently — useful for mixed multi-monitor setups.

Ambient light aware

On devices with a light sensor, daytime brightness can nudge to match the room, on top of the schedule — off by default, like every optional feature here.

A comfort tool, not a medical device

Monitor Wellness is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical care. If you experience migraines, please talk to a doctor about treatment — use this as one comfort tool alongside that care, not instead of it. Migraine Mode activates instantly (never a flash or strobe) and always fades back off over about 20 seconds by design, since sudden brightness changes can themselves be uncomfortable.
